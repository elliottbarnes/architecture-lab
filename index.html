A DIFFERENT KIND OF COMPUTER

No GPU. No hard drive.
A surprisingly small world.

The original Macintosh shared its RAM between programs, the screen image, and sound data. Its 68000 had no on-chip cache or memory-management unit. The floppy was its built-in mass storage; much of its interface machinery lived permanently in ROM.

This is an interactive architectural illustration, with a working bitmap experiment and guided signal stories. It does not emulate the 68000, boot a Macintosh ROM, or reproduce electrical timing.
